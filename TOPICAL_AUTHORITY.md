# Topical Authority — film.kaca.co.id

## Role and boundary

Film.kaca.co.id is a decision-support and service-education property for architectural window film in Indonesia. It should help homeowners, facility teams, designers, and procurement staff choose, specify, install, inspect, and maintain film for buildings. Existing service routes own transactional intent; articles must not invent product construction, brand equivalence, measured performance, glass compatibility, compliance, warranty, or price claims. Those claims require current manufacturer documents, recognized test methods, glass-system review, written installer terms, or a dated quotation as appropriate.

## Internal-link rule

Every article links to its parent hub and the nearest next decision. Fundamentals link to metric and compatibility explainers; application pages link to survey and selection; specification pages link to installation acceptance; diagnosis pages link to care, repair, removal, or warranty as appropriate. Educational pages may link to an existing service route only when the reader is ready for a survey or quotation, never as a substitute for evidence.
